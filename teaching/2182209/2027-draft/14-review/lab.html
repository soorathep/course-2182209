Review stations

About 60 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

Three stations review Classes 1 to 13. Work through them in any order your instructor gives; each takes about 20 minutes. Write down which class each formula came from. The next step stays locked until you finish the one before it; after two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. The expected values come from review_checks.py, computed in advance. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D, or run review_checks.py in desktop Python.

The lock order is Station 1, 2, 3. If your group starts at Station 2 or 3, read and work it on paper first, then enter the answers when you reach it here.
