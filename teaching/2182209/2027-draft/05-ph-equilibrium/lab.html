pH and a shifting equilibrium

About 30 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

You compare acidities on the pH scale, find [OH−] from Kw, dilute an acid until water takes over, and shift a counting model of equilibrium. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. After two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. The expected values come from ph_lab.py, computed in advance. The two sliders do small calculations in JavaScript with the same formulas as the Python. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D, or run ph_lab.py in desktop Python.

Write small numbers as 1e-11 (meaning 1 × 10−11). Studying on your own? Watch the narrated slides for Class 5 first.
