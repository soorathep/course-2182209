The double layer and supercapacitors

The question for Class 11 3 min

From Class 7 to Class 10, electrons crossed from an electrode into an ion by a reaction. Class 11 stores charge at a surface with no reaction at all. That is how a supercapacitor works, and how every bit of DRAM memory in a computer holds a 1 or a 0.

What a capacitor is 7 min

A capacitor is two conductors separated by an insulator. Push electrons onto one side and the other side loses the same number: the two sides carry +Q and −Q. Nothing crosses the insulator.

Q = C × V: the charge stored is the capacitance times the voltage

Capacitance C is in farads: 1 F = 1 C/V. One farad is large; circuit capacitors are usually microfarads (µF, 10−6 F) or picofarads (pF, 10−12 F).

For two flat plates of area A a distance d apart, with an insulator of permittivity ε between them:

C = ε A / d: more area and a smaller gap give more capacitance

Energy in a capacitor 5 min

Charging a capacitor stores energy E = ½ C V². Note the square: doubling the voltage stores four times the energy.

Example. A 0.200 F capacitor at 1.0 V holds Q = 0.200 C and E = ½ × 0.200 × 1.0² = 0.100 J.

The double layer, in one paragraph 5 min

Put a metal or carbon electrode in an electrolyte and make it negative. Cations from the solution crowd against the surface to balance the charge. The result is two thin layers of opposite charge, less than a nanometer apart: the electric double layer. It behaves like a capacitor with a tiny gap d, and porous carbon gives it an enormous area A. Class 11 shows why that combination gives farads in a device the size of a coin.

Check yourself 5 min

Write your answers first, then open each one.

1. A capacitor of 0.50 F is charged to 2.0 V. How much charge does it hold?

AnswerQ = CV = 1.0 C.

2. And how much energy?

Answer½ × 0.50 × 2.0² = 1.0 J.

3. What happens to C if the gap d is halved?

AnswerIt doubles: C = εA/d.

4. 0.010 A flows for 20 s. How much charge? (Class 10)

Answer0.20 C.

5. In a capacitor, do electrons cross the insulator?

AnswerNo. Charge builds up on each side.

6. Is SiO2 a conductor or an insulator? (Classes 2 and 6)

AnswerAn insulator. It is the layer between the plates of capacitors on a chip.

If you got 5 or 6 right, you are ready. If not, reread the section on what a capacitor is.
