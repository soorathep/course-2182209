Measuring a capacitance

About 30 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

Two supercapacitors, A and B, are charged at the same constant current. You read their capacitance from the voltage data, then work out the charge and energy, and see what changing the capacitance does. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. After two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. The data and expected values come from edlc_lab.py, computed in advance. The data are synthetic teaching data. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D, or run edlc_lab.py in desktop Python.

Studying on your own? Watch the narrated slides for Class 11 first.
