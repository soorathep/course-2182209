Reading a Pourbaix diagram

About 30 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

You read a Pourbaix diagram for Cu in water: which form of Cu is stable at a given pH and potential, where the boundaries lie at pH 9, and what the diagram cannot tell you. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. After two tries the expected answer is shown.

What runs where. The diagram and the expected answers come from pourbaix_lab.py, computed in advance. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D, or run pourbaix_lab.py in desktop Python.

Studying on your own? Watch the narrated slides for Class 13 first.
