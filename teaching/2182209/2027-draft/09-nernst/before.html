The question for Class 9 3 min

Class 7 put the metals in an order, and Class 8 said the Daniell cell gives about 1.10 V. Class 9 turns the order into numbers, explains the 1.10 V, links it to the Gibbs energy of the reaction, and shows how the potential shifts when the concentration changes. All of this matters in a plating bath, where the Cu2+ concentration falls as Cu is used.

Voltage is energy per charge 5 min

A volt is a joule per coulomb: 1 V = 1 J/C. A cell of 1.10 V gives each coulomb of charge that passes through the wire up to 1.10 J of energy.

One mole of electrons carries a charge called the Faraday constant:

F = NA × e = 6.022 × 1023 × 1.602 × 10−19 C = 96 485 C/mol

So when 2 mol of electrons pass through a 1.10 V cell, the electrical work is at most 2 × 96 485 × 1.10 = 212 000 J = 212 kJ.

A potential needs a reference 6 min

A voltmeter measures a difference between two electrodes, never one alone. So one half-reaction is chosen as the zero: the standard hydrogen electrode (SHE), 2H+ + 2e− → H2, set to 0 V.

The standard reduction potential E° of any half-reaction is measured against the SHE, written as a reduction, at 1 M, 1 bar and 25 °C:

Half-reactionE°, V
Ag+ + e− → Ag+0.80
Cu2+ + 2e− → Cu+0.34
2H+ + 2e− → H20.00
Fe2+ + 2e− → Fe−0.44
Zn2+ + 2e− → Zn−0.76
Al3+ + 3e− → Al−1.66

The higher E°, the more the ion wants electrons. It is Class 7's order of metals, upside down.

One equation to preview 5 min

For two half-cells, E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode. Daniell: 0.34 − (−0.76) = 1.10 V. A positive E°cell means the reaction goes on its own.

In class you will also use base-10 logarithms again (Class 5): log10(0.01) = −2. The Nernst equation shifts E by 0.0592/n V for each factor of 10 in concentration.

Check yourself 5 min

Write your answers first, then open each one.

1. What does 1 V mean in joules and coulombs?

Answer1 J of energy per coulomb of charge.

2. What is the charge of 1 mol of electrons?

AnswerF = 96 485 C.

3. Why do we need the SHE?

AnswerA voltmeter only measures differences, so one half-reaction is set to 0 V as the reference.

4. Which ion wants electrons more, Cu2+ or Zn2+?

AnswerCu2+: its E° is higher (+0.34 against −0.76 V).

5. Find E°cell for the Daniell cell.

Answer0.34 − (−0.76) = 1.10 V.
