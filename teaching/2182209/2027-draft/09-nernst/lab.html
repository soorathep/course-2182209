How concentration moves a potential

About 30 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

You see how the potential of a Cu electrode moves when the Cu2+ solution is diluted, then calculate the standard potential and Gibbs energy of the Daniell cell, and its potential away from 1 M. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. After two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. The expected values come from nernst_lab.py, computed in advance. The slider uses the same equation in JavaScript. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D, or run nernst_lab.py in desktop Python.

Give potentials to 4 decimals (0.0001 V). Studying on your own? Watch the narrated slides for Class 9 first.
