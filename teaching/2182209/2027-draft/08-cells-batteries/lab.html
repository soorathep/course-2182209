Following electrons and ions in a cell

About 30 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

You follow the electrons and the ions in three cells: the Daniell cell, a Li-ion cell in discharge and in charge, and a zinc-air cell. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. After two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. The expected answers come from cell_lab.py, computed in advance. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D, or run cell_lab.py in desktop Python.
