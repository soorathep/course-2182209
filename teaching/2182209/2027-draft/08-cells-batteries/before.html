The question for Class 8 3 min

In Class 7, Zn gave its electrons straight to Cu2+ in one beaker, and the energy was lost as a little heat. If the two half-reactions happen in two separate places joined by a wire, the electrons must travel through the wire and can light a bulb or run a phone. That arrangement is an electrochemical cell, and a battery is one or more cells.

Two electrodes, two paths 7 min

A cell has two electrodes (electron conductors, such as metal strips) in contact with an electrolyte (an ion conductor, such as a salt solution, Class 4).

Anode: where oxidation happens. Cathode: where reduction happens.

In the Daniell cell, a Zn strip sits in ZnSO4 and a Cu strip in CuSO4:

Anode (Zn): Zn → Zn2+ + 2e−
Cathode (Cu): Cu2+ + 2e− → Cu

Electrons flow through the wire from the anode to the cathode. A salt bridge, a tube of KNO3 solution, joins the two solutions so that ions can move between them. Electrons never cross the electrolyte, and ions never travel through the wire.

Why the ions must move 5 min

Every solution must stay neutral (Class 4). As the cell runs, the Zn side gains Zn2+ and the Cu side loses Cu2+. Without a path for ions, the Zn side would become positive and the Cu side negative, and these charges would stop the electrons within moments. In the salt bridge, anions (NO3−) move toward the anode side and cations (K+) toward the cathode side. In a battery, a porous separator soaked in electrolyte does the same job.

Cell notation, and two kinds of cell 5 min

The Daniell cell is written Zn | Zn2+(aq) || Cu2+(aq) | Cu: anode on the left, a single line for a phase boundary, a double line for the salt bridge.

A galvanic cell runs on its own and does work (a battery in use). An electrolytic cell is driven by a power supply (a battery being charged, or Cu electroplating, where the wafer is the cathode).

Check yourself 5 min

Write your answers first, then open each one.

1. Is the anode where oxidation or reduction happens?

AnswerOxidation.

2. In the Daniell cell, which metal is the anode?

AnswerZn: Zn → Zn2+ + 2e−.

3. Which way do electrons flow in the wire?

AnswerFrom the anode (Zn) to the cathode (Cu).

4. Do electrons pass through the salt bridge?

AnswerNo. Ions move there; electrons stay in the electrodes and wire.

5. What is the oxidation number of O in O2 and in OH−? (Class 7)

Answer0 and −2.

6. Is Cu electroplating a galvanic or an electrolytic cell?

AnswerElectrolytic: a power supply drives it.

If you got 5 or 6 right, you are ready. If not, reread the section on the two paths.
