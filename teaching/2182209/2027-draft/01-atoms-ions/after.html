Two more worked examples

1. Average atomic mass of boron. Natural B is 19.9% B-10 (10.0129 u) and 80.1% B-11 (11.0093 u). The average weights each isotope mass by its fraction of atoms:

M(B) = 0.199 × 10.0129 + 0.801 × 11.0093 = 1.993 + 8.818 = 10.81 u

The result lies nearer 11 because most atoms are B-11. Boron is the dopant that makes p-type Si in Class 6.

2. Charge balance in Al2O3. Oxide is O2−. Let q be the charge on each Al:

2 × q + 3 × (−2) = 0, so q = +3

Each Al3+ has 13 − 3 = 10 electrons. Al2O3 is an insulator used in chips and as a coating on battery electrodes.

Three mistakes to avoid

  1. "A positive ion has more electrons." It has fewer. Check with charge = protons − electrons.
  2. "Cu2+ is a different element from Cu." The element is set by Z. Only electrons changed.
  3. "The atomic mass of Cu should be a whole number." It is an average over isotopes, weighted by how many atoms of each there are.

Practice

Work on paper. The first four repeat the class; the rest go one step further.

1. Give the protons and electrons of Al3+ (Z = 13) and S2− (Z = 16).

AnswerAl3+: 13 protons, 10 electrons. S2−: 16 protons, 18 electrons.

2. An ion has 26 protons and 23 electrons. Name it. (Z = 26 is iron, Fe.)

Answer26 − 23 = +3: Fe3+.

3. How many neutrons are in 28Si and in 31P? (Z: Si 14, P 15)

Answer28 − 14 = 14 and 31 − 15 = 16.

4. A student writes that Na+ has 12 electrons. Find the error.

AnswerA positive ion has fewer electrons than its atom: 11 − 1 = 10, not 12. With 12 electrons the charge would be −1.

5. How many electrons are in one sulfate ion, SO42−? (Z: S 16, O 8)

AnswerAtoms: 16 + 4 × 8 = 48. The 2− charge adds 2 electrons: 50.

6. How many electrons are in one CuSO4 unit? Check your answer two ways.

AnswerAtom by atom: 29 + 16 + 4 × 8 = 77. As ions: Cu2+ (27) + SO42− (50) = 77. The same, because the unit is neutral.

7. Modern transistors use hafnium oxide, HfO2, as the gate insulator. Find the charge on Hf and its electrons. (Z(Hf) = 72)

Answerq + 2 × (−2) = 0, so Hf4+, with 72 − 4 = 68 electrons.

8. Natural Cu is 69.15% Cu-63 (62.9296 u) and the rest Cu-65 (64.9278 u). If a sample were 50% of each, would its average mass be higher or lower than 63.546 u? Calculate it.

AnswerHigher, because more of the heavier isotope: 0.5 × 62.9296 + 0.5 × 64.9278 = 63.929 u.
