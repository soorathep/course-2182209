Two more worked examples

1. How long to plate 2.00 µm of Cu at 15 mA/cm²? Work per 1 cm², at 100% efficiency.

Mass per cm² = thickness × density = 2.00 × 10−4 cm × 8.96 g/cm³ = 1.79 × 10−3 g

n(Cu) = 1.79 × 10−3 / 63.55 = 2.82 × 10−5 mol; n(e−) = 5.64 × 10−5 mol

Q = 5.64 × 10−5 × 96 485 = 5.44 C per cm²; t = Q / j = 5.44 / 0.015 = 363 s, about 6 min

The answer does not depend on the wafer size, only on current density and time.

2. Specific capacity of a Si anode. Fully lithiated Si takes 3.75 Li per Si (Li15Si4), so z = 3.75 per Si atom:

q = zF / (3.6 M) = 3.75 × 96 485 / (3.6 × 28.09) = 3580 mAh/g

That is almost ten times graphite's 372 mAh/g, which is why Si is added to Li-ion anodes. The catch is that Si swells by about 300% when full of Li.

Three mistakes to avoid

  1. Using minutes in Q = It. Time must be in seconds for coulombs.
  2. Forgetting z. Cu2+ needs 2 electrons per atom: divide n(e−) by 2.
  3. "More mAh means more energy." Energy is Ah × V. Compare batteries of different voltage in Wh.

Practice

Work on paper. The first four repeat the class; the rest go one step further. F = 96 485 C/mol; Cu 63.55 g/mol, 8.96 g/cm³.

1. 0.100 A for 600 s at 90%: find the mass of Cu.

Answer0.90 × 6.22 × 10−4 / 2 × 63.55 = 17.78 mg.

2. That Cu covers 10.0 cm². How thick is it?

Answer0.01778 g / (8.96 × 10.0) = 1.985 × 10−4 cm = 1.985 µm.

3. The area doubles at the same current and time. What happens to the mass and thickness?

AnswerMass the same; thickness halves.

4. A 3.0 Ah battery supplies 0.300 A on average. How long does it last?

Answer10 h.

5. How long does 2.00 µm of Cu take at 15 mA/cm² and 100% efficiency?

Answer5.44 C/cm² ÷ 0.015 A/cm² = 363 s, about 6 min.

6. Find the specific capacity of LiFePO4 (z = 1, M = 157.8 g/mol).

Answer96 485 / (3.6 × 157.8) = 170 mAh/g.

7. A phone battery is 4500 mAh at 3.85 V. A power bank is 20 000 mAh at 3.7 V. Find each in Wh. May the power bank go in an airplane cabin without approval (limit 100 Wh)?

AnswerPhone 17.3 Wh; power bank 74 Wh: yes, below 100 Wh.

8. A bath plates at 85% efficiency. What current plates 1.00 µm on 706.9 cm² (a 300 mm wafer) in 300 s?

AnswerMass = 1.00 × 10−4 × 8.96 × 706.9 = 0.633 g = 9.97 × 10−3 mol Cu; electrons needed 1.99 × 10−2 mol = 1923 C; at 85%, Q = 1923 / 0.85 = 2263 C; I = 2263 / 300 = 7.5 A.
