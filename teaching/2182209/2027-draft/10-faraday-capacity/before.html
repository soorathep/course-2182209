The question for Class 10 3 min

A plating tool runs a current through a wafer for a set time. How much Cu does it deposit, and how thick? A phone battery says 4500 mAh. What is that a measure of? Both answers come from one idea: an electric current is a flow of electrons, and electrons can be counted in moles (Class 3) using F (Class 9).

Current, charge and time 6 min

Current I is charge per time, in amperes: 1 A = 1 C/s. So the charge that passes in a time t is

Q = I × t (C = A × s)

Example. 0.100 A for 10.0 min = 600 s: Q = 0.100 × 600 = 60.0 C.

Always convert the time to seconds first.

From charge to moles of metal 8 min

One mole of electrons is 96 485 C (F, Class 9). So

n(e−) = Q / F, and n(metal) = n(e−) / z

where z is the number of electrons per metal ion: z = 2 for Cu2+ + 2e− → Cu (Class 7).

Example. 60.0 C: n(e−) = 60.0 / 96 485 = 6.22 × 10−4 mol. n(Cu) = 3.11 × 10−4 mol. Mass = 3.11 × 10−4 × 63.55 = 0.0198 g = 19.8 mg.

In class you will add two more steps: the thickness of the film, using the density of Cu (8.96 g/cm³) and the area, and the current efficiency, the fraction of the charge that actually plates Cu.

Charge and energy on a battery 5 min

A battery's capacity is a charge, written in ampere-hours: 1 Ah = 1 A × 3600 s = 3600 C. A 4500 mAh phone battery can deliver 4.5 A for 1 h, or 0.45 A for 10 h.

Energy is charge times voltage (Class 9: 1 V = 1 J/C). In watt-hours, Wh = Ah × V.

Check yourself 5 min

Write your answers first, then open each one.

1. 2.00 A flows for 5.00 min. How much charge?

Answer2.00 × 300 = 600 C.

2. How many moles of electrons is 965 C?

Answer965 / 96 485 = 0.0100 mol.

3. How many moles of Cu do 0.0100 mol of electrons deposit?

Answer0.00500 mol: 2 electrons per Cu.

4. How many coulombs is 1.00 Ah?

Answer3600 C.

5. A 2.0 Ah cell works at 3.7 V. How much energy does it store?

Answer2.0 × 3.7 = 7.4 Wh.

Answer63.55 g/mol.
