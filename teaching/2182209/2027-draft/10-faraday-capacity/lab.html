Counting charge: plating and capacity

About 30 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

You plate Cu on paper: from current and time to charge, moles, mass and thickness. Then you change one condition at a time, and finish with battery capacity and energy. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. After two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. The expected values come from faraday_lab.py, computed in advance. The sliders use the same formulas in JavaScript. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D, or run faraday_lab.py in desktop Python.

Write small numbers as 6.22e-4. Studying on your own? Watch the narrated slides for Class 10 first.
