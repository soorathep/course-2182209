Choosing materials for a chip with numbers

About 30 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

A chip needs a conductor, a semiconductor and an insulator. You classify bonds from electronegativity, read a resistivity table, calculate the resistance of a chip wire and change its metal and width, count atoms in unit cells, and compare two forms of SiO2. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. Numbers are accepted within about 1%; after two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. The tables come from bonding_lab.py, computed in advance. The slider in Task 3 does one small calculation in JavaScript, R = ρL/A, the same formula as the Python. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D on a slide marked “D · Python”, or run bonding_lab.py in desktop Python.
