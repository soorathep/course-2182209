Doping a silicon wafer

About 30 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

You decide what P and B do in Si, check that doped Si is neutral, find how few dopant atoms are needed, and calculate how they change the carriers and the resistivity. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. After two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. The expected values come from doping_lab.py, computed in advance. The slider does a small calculation in JavaScript with the same formulas as the Python. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D, or run doping_lab.py in desktop Python.

Write large or small numbers as 2.1e4 (meaning 2.1 × 104). Studying on your own? Watch the narrated slides for Class 6 first.
