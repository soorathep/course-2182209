Making and diluting a CuSO4 bath

About 30 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

Copper wiring is plated from a CuSO4 bath. You check amounts and concentrations, plan a dilution, weigh the crystals for a bath, and find the ions it contains. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. After two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. The expected values come from solutions_lab.py, computed in advance. The sliders in Task 3 do one small calculation in JavaScript, m = C V M, the same as the Python. No Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D, or run solutions_lab.py in desktop Python.
