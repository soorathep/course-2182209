Solutions, concentration and electrolytes

About 30 minutes. Read it before Class 4, try the questions, and bring what is still unclear.
Download PDFSlidesLabAfter class

The question for Class 4 3 min

Copper wires on a chip are grown from a liquid: a plating bath of CuSO4 and sulfuric acid in water. To make that bath, check it and dilute it, you need concentration. Class 4 also explains why the bath conducts while solid NaCl (Class 2) does not.

Solute, solvent, solution 4 min

The solute is what dissolves (CuSO4); the solvent does the dissolving (water); together they make the solution. A solution in water is written with (aq), for aqueous: CuSO4(aq).

Concentration in mol/L 7 min

C = n / V, with n in mol and V the volume of the whole solution in liters

The unit mol/L is also written M (molar). So 0.100 M CuSO4 holds 0.100 mol of CuSO4 in every liter of solution.

Example. 0.0100 mol of CuSO4 made up to 100.0 mL: C = 0.0100 mol ÷ 0.1000 L = 0.100 mol/L.

Turned around, n = C × V: 40.0 mL of 0.0500 M holds 0.0500 × 0.0400 = 0.00200 mol.

Always convert mL to L first: 100.0 mL = 0.1000 L.

Dilution keeps the moles 6 min

Adding water spreads the same solute through more volume. The amount n does not change; the concentration falls.

C1V1 = C2V2 (stock before = diluted after)

Example. How much 0.100 M stock makes 50.0 mL of 0.0200 M? V1 = 0.0200 × 50.0 / 0.100 = 10.0 mL. Put 10.0 mL of stock in a 50 mL flask, then add water up to the mark. V2 is the final volume, not the water you add.

Salts give ions in water 5 min

When a salt dissolves, its ions separate and move freely:

CuSO4(s) → Cu2+(aq) + SO42−(aq)
Na2SO4(s) → 2 Na+(aq) + SO42−(aq)

A substance that gives ions in water is an electrolyte. The moving ions carry current, so the solution conducts. Every solution is electrically neutral: the positive and negative charges always balance.

Check yourself 5 min

Write your answers first, then open each one.

1. 0.0200 mol of solute is made up to 250 mL. What is C?

Answer0.0200 / 0.250 = 0.0800 mol/L.

2. How many moles are in 40.0 mL of 0.0500 M CuSO4?

Answer0.0500 × 0.0400 = 0.00200 mol.

3. Water is added to a solution. Which changes: n, C, or both?

AnswerOnly C. The amount n stays the same.

4. How much 0.100 M stock makes 50.0 mL of 0.0200 M?

Answer10.0 mL, then fill to the 50.0 mL mark.

5. What ions does Na2SO4 give in water, and how many of each per formula unit?

Answer2 Na+ and 1 SO42−.

6. Why does salt water conduct when solid salt does not?

AnswerIn water the ions are free to move and carry charge.

If you got 5 or 6 right, you are ready. If not, reread the sections on concentration and dilution.
